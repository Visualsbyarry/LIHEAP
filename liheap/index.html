About LIHEAP

Focused on keeping homes safe, affordable, and liveable.

LIHEAP is one of the most important tools in the social safety net, helping households facing difficult energy costs reduce financial stress and avoid critical utility disruptions.

The program delivers targeted assistance to households with significant home energy needs, especially during periods of high heating or cooling costs.

6.7M+

Targeted

Assistance is directed toward households facing immediate energy-related hardship and crisis conditions.

Efficient

State block grants move quickly to local agencies so support can reach families when it is needed most.
